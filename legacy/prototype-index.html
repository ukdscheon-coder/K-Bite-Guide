Bibimbap

비빔밥 (Bibimbap)
Bowl of warm white rice topped with seasoned vegetables, chili pepper paste (gochujang), and egg.
Vegetarian Option Available

Bulgogi

불고기 (Bulgogi)
Marinated thinly sliced beef grilled with sweet savory soy sauce, garlic, and sesame oil.
Savory / Non-Spicy

Samgyetang

삼계탕 (Samgye-tang)
Ginseng chicken soup made with a whole young chicken stuffed with glutinous rice, ginseng, and jujubes.
Health Food / Nourishing

Tteokbokki

떡볶이 (Tteok-bokki)
Simmered cylindrical rice cakes cooked in spicy-sweet gochujang chili sauce with fish cakes.
Spicy Street Food

Pajeon

파전 (Green Onion Pancake)
Savory Korean pancake made with scallions, batter, and dipping sauce.
Vegetarian / Non-Spicy

Galbitang

갈비탕 (Beef Short Rib Soup)
Clear, hearty soup made with simmered beef short ribs, radish, and glass noodles.
Clear Broth
